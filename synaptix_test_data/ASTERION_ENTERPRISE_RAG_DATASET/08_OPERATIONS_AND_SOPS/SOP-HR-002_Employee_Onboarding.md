# SOP — Employee Onboarding

**Document ID:** SOP-HR-002  
**Version:** 2.1 | **Status:** Approved | **Effective:** 2025-01-01  
**Owner:** HR Manager | **Org:** ORG-ASTERION-001

## 1. Purpose
Ensure new joiners receive accounts, equipment, training, and policy acknowledgments before or on day one.

## 2. Ordered Steps (summary)
1. Offer accepted → HR creates employee record (status: Pre-join).
2. T-7 days: IT provisions accounts per role template; Manager confirms start date.
3. T-3 days: Security assigns baseline access; Restricted access only if pre-approved.
4. Day 1: Orientation, policy acknowledgment (Code of Conduct, Security, AI), equipment handover.
5. Day 1–5: Role-specific training checklist completed by Manager.
6. Day 30/60/90: Probation check-ins per POL-HR probation rules.
7. Confirmation: HR updates status to Confirmed; full AL usability unlocked per POL-HR-001.
