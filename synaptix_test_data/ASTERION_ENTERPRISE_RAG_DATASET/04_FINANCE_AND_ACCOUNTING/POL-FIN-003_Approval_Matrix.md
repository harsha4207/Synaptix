## 1. Purpose
Establish binding monetary approval thresholds for expenses, purchase orders, vendor payments, and budget adjustments so that every financial commitment has a named, authorized approver.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all departments and all locations. Emergency exceptions are defined in Section 7.

## 3. Definitions
| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| Transaction Value | Total INR amount of the commitment including taxes where known |
| Cost Center Owner | Manager or above assigned to the cost center in the finance system |
| Budgeted | Amount is within the approved annual/departmental budget line |
| Unbudgeted | Amount exceeds or is outside the approved budget line |

## 4. Approval Thresholds — Expense Claims and Reimbursements
| Transaction Value (INR) | Approver (Budgeted) | Approver (Unbudgeted) | Secondary / Notes |
|-------------------------|---------------------|-----------------------|-------------------|
| ≤ 10,000 | Direct Manager | Direct Manager + Finance Manager | Auto-route if system-validated receipts |
| 10,001 – 50,000 | Direct Manager | Department Head + Finance Manager | |
| 50,001 – 100,000 | Department Head | Department Head + Finance Director | |
| 100,001 – 500,000 | Finance Director | CFO | |
| 500,001 – 2,000,000 | CFO | CFO + CEO | |

## 5. Approval Thresholds — Purchase Orders and Vendor Commitments
| Transaction Value (INR) | Approver | Additional Requirement |
|-------------------------|----------|------------------------|
| ≤ 25,000 | Cost Center Owner (Manager+) | Valid vendor in master; PO created |
| 25,001 – 100,000 | Department Head | Competitive quote if > INR 50,000 (min 2 quotes) |
| 100,001 – 500,000 | Finance Director or Head of Procurement | Min 3 quotes or approved single-source justification |
| 500,001 – 2,000,000 | CFO | Legal review of contract terms if MSA not in place |
| > 2,000,000 | CEO | Legal + Procurement + Finance concurrence |

## 6. Segregation of Duties
- Requestor cannot be the sole approver of their own expense or PO.
- AP processor cannot approve the same invoice they entered.
- Vendor master creation requires dual control (Procurement + Finance).

## 7. Emergency Exceptions
When delay would cause material business harm (production outage, safety, contractual penalty):
1. Verbal/email approval from the next available approver at or above the required tier.
2. Formal system entry and documentation within **2 business days**.
3. All emergency approvals reported to Finance Director weekly.

## 8. Budget Variance Authority
- Cost Center Owner may reallocate within their cost center up to 5% of the annual budget line without escalation.
- Variances > 5% or any capital expenditure require Finance Manager review.
- Forecast variances > 10% of department annual budget escalate to CFO.
