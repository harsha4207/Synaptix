## 1. Purpose
Define eligibility, accrual, usage, carry-forward, encashment, and approval rules for all leave types so that managers, employees, and automated systems apply consistent rules.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all locations (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad) and all employment classifications listed in Section 4. Does not apply to third-party vendor personnel unless a separate written agreement incorporates these rules.

## 5. Annual Leave (AL)
### 5.1 Entitlement
- Full-time permanent employees: **18 working days** per Leave Year.
- Accrual: 1.5 days per completed calendar month of service.
- New joiners: Pro-rata from joining date; first accrual credited at end of joining month if joined on or before the 15th, otherwise from next month.

### 5.2 Carry-Forward
- Maximum carry-forward into next Leave Year: **8 days**.
- Excess above 8 days lapses on 31 March unless an exception is approved in writing by CHRO before 15 March.

### 5.3 Encashment
- Encashment is permitted only on separation (resignation, retirement, or termination) for unutilized AL up to the carry-forward cap + current-year pro-rata balance.
- Encashment rate: Basic salary / 30 × number of days.
- No mid-year encashment while in service.

### 5.4 Approval Authority for AL
| Duration | Approver | Alternate if Approver Unavailable |
|----------|----------|-----------------------------------|
| 1–3 consecutive working days | Direct Manager | Skip-level Manager |
| 4–10 consecutive working days | Direct Manager + HR Manager acknowledgment | Department Head |
| >10 consecutive working days | Department Head + HR Manager | CHRO |

Application must be submitted in the HR system at least 7 calendar days in advance for planned AL of 4+ days, except documented emergencies.

## 6. Casual Leave (CL)
- Entitlement: **12 days** per Leave Year for full-time permanent employees.
- Maximum continuous CL: 3 working days.
- Does not carry forward; unused CL lapses on 31 March.
- Approval: Direct Manager (or Skip-level if Manager unavailable). Same-day CL requires telephonic/email notification before shift start plus system entry within 24 hours.

## 7. Sick Leave (SL)
- Entitlement: **12 days** per Leave Year.
- Medical certificate required for SL of 3 or more consecutive working days.
- Does not carry forward.
- Approval: Direct Manager; HR must be informed for absences >3 days.

## 8. Maternity and Paternity Leave
- Maternity Leave: 26 weeks for the birth parent (as per applicable Indian law framework for the synthetic policy design). Application to HR at least 8 weeks before expected delivery where foreseeable.
- Paternity Leave: 5 working days within 3 months of childbirth. Approval: Direct Manager + HR notification.

## 9. Compensatory Off
- Granted only for pre-approved extra hours on weekends or public holidays, documented by Manager.
- Must be utilized within 60 days of the extra work date; otherwise lapses.
- Approval: Same as AL for the corresponding duration.

## 10. Public Holidays
- Published annually by HR before 15 March for the next Leave Year.
- Locations may have limited local holiday variations approved by CHRO.

## 11. Leave During Notice Period
- AL may be availed during notice only with written approval of Department Head and HR Manager.
- Company may require the employee to remain available; unapproved absence during notice may be treated as leave without pay or misconduct under POL-HR-005.

## 12. Exceptions
- CHRO may grant exceptional leave (paid or unpaid) for documented extraordinary circumstances. All exceptions must be recorded with reason and duration.

## 13. Compliance Monitoring
- HR runs monthly leave-balance reconciliation.
- Managers are responsible for timely approval or rejection (SLA: 3 business days for planned leave).

## 14. Consequences of Non-Compliance
- Unauthorized absence may be marked Leave Without Pay and, if repeated, may initiate disciplinary process under POL-HR-005.
