# Disciplinary Procedure

**Version:** 2.2 | **Status:** Approved | **Effective:** 2025-01-01 | **Review:** 2026-12-31  
**Owner:** CHRO | **Approver:** CEO | **Classification:** Internal | **Org:** ORG-ASTERION-001

## 1. Purpose
Provide a fair, documented process for addressing misconduct and performance failures.

## 2. Principles
- Progressive discipline where appropriate (coaching → written warning → final warning → termination).
- Serious misconduct (theft, violence, willful data breach, harassment) may proceed directly to investigation and potential summary action.
- Employee has right to be informed of allegations and to respond before a final decision.

## 3. Process Steps
1. Manager identifies issue and consults HR Manager within 2 business days.
2. HR determines whether informal resolution or formal investigation is required.
3. If formal: written notice to employee; investigation by HR or designated investigator (max 15 business days unless extended with CHRO approval).
4. Findings report; employee response opportunity (5 business days).
5. Decision by Manager + HR Manager (or CHRO for severe cases); recorded in employee file.
6. Appeal: one level up within 7 business days of decision communication.
