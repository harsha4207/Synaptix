## 3. Department Catalog

### 3.1 Executive Management (DEPT-EXEC-001)
- **Owner Role:** CEO
- **Responsibilities:** Strategy, capital allocation, external representation, ELT leadership
- **Headcount (synthetic):** 8

### 3.2 Finance (DEPT-FIN-001)
- **Owner Role:** CFO
- **Responsibilities:** Budgeting, financial reporting, AP/AR, treasury, internal financial controls, month-end/year-end close
- **Headcount:** ~35
- **Key Policies:** POL-FIN-001 Budget Policy, POL-FIN-002 Expense Reimbursement, POL-FIN-003 Approval Matrix

## 4. Decision-Making Principles
1. Decisions are made at the lowest competent level consistent with the applicable approval matrix.
2. Material risk, legal, or security decisions escalate to the named owner or ELT.
3. Cross-functional decisions require documented concurrence from all affected department owners.
4. AI systems may recommend; humans approve and remain accountable.
