# Working Hours and Attendance Policy

**Document ID:** POL-HR-002  
**Version:** 3.1 | **Status:** Approved | **Effective:** 2025-04-01 | **Review:** 2026-03-31  
**Owner:** CHRO | **Approver:** CEO | **Classification:** Internal | **Org:** ORG-ASTERION-001

## 1. Purpose
Define standard working hours, core hours, attendance recording, and hybrid/remote arrangements.

## 2. Standard Working Hours
- Full-time employees: 40 hours per week, Monday–Friday.
- Core hours (all locations): 11:00–16:00 India Standard Time — employees must be available for meetings and collaboration unless on approved leave.
- Flexible start: between 08:00 and 10:30; corresponding end time to complete 8 hours excluding lunch break (minimum 30 minutes).

## 3. Attendance Recording
- All employees must record attendance via the approved HR system or biometric device where installed.
- Failure to record for 3 consecutive days without Manager acknowledgment escalates to HR.

## 4. Hybrid and Remote Work
- Eligible roles (determined by Department Head + HR): up to 2 remote days per week by default after successful completion of probation.
- Fully remote arrangements require written approval of Department Head, CHRO, and (for roles with customer data access) CISO.
- Remote employees remain subject to the same leave, conduct, and security policies.

## 5. Overtime
- Individual Contributors in non-exempt categories (as classified by HR): pre-approved overtime compensated as Compensatory Off (see POL-HR-001) or as per applicable wage rules.
- Managers and above: no overtime pay; exceptional extra effort recognized via performance process.
