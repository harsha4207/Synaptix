## Chief Financial Officer (ROLE-CFO-001)
- **Reports to:** CEO
- **Primary Accountabilities:** Financial planning, reporting integrity, cash management, internal financial controls, investor/board financial packs (synthetic)
- **Approval Authority:** Up to INR 5,000,000 per transaction (see POL-FIN-003)
- **Key Relationships:** Finance Director, Controllers, External Auditor (synthetic)

## Finance Manager (ROLE-MGR-FIN-001)
- **Reports to:** Finance Director
- **Primary Accountabilities:** Month-end close coordination, expense claim review, budget variance reporting, AP supervision
- **Approval Authority:** Up to INR 100,000 for expense and AP items within approved budget
- **Leave Approval:** Yes — for direct reports

## Individual Contributor (ROLE-EMP-001)
- **Reports to:** Designated Manager
- **Primary Accountabilities:** Delivery of assigned work within role description; compliance with all policies
- **Approval Authority:** None for financial or leave of others
- **Access Baseline:** Internal classification systems required for role; Confidential/Restricted only with documented business need and manager + data-owner approval
