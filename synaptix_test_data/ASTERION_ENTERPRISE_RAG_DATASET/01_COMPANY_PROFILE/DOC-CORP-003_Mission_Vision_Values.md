## Mission (Canonical Statement)
To enable enterprises to make faster, more reliable decisions by delivering governed, AI-assisted software and analytics platforms that respect data privacy, regulatory obligations, and operational accountability.

## Vision (Canonical Statement)
To become the trusted partner for mid-to-large enterprises in India and selected international markets that require transparent, auditable, and human-supervised AI-enabled business systems.

## Values — Behavioral Expectations
| Value | Expected Behavior | Prohibited Behavior |
|-------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Integrity | Report accurate metrics; escalate data quality issues | Fabricating results or concealing errors |
| Accountability | Document decisions and owners | Diffusing ownership without escalation path |
| Customer Stewardship | Protect customer data; meet SLA commitments | Sharing customer data without authorization |
| Continuous Learning | Complete assigned training; share post-mortems | Ignoring mandatory compliance training |
| Governed Innovation | Follow AI and change-management policies | Deploying unapproved models or tools into production |
