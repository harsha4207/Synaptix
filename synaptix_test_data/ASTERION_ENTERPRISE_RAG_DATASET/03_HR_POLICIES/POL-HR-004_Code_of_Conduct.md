# Code of Conduct

**Document ID:** POL-HR-004  
**Version:** 3.0 | **Status:** Approved | **Effective:** 2024-10-01 | **Review:** 2026-09-30  
**Owner:** CHRO | **Approver:** CEO | **Classification:** Internal | **Org:** ORG-ASTERION-001

## 1. Purpose
Set behavioral expectations for all persons engaged by Asterion (employees, interns, contractors while on assignment).

## 2. Core Rules
1. Comply with all applicable laws and company policies.
2. Protect Confidential and Restricted information; share only under authorized need-to-know.
3. Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect; harassment and discrimination are prohibited (see separate Workplace Harassment Prevention procedure referenced in POL-HR-005).
4. Avoid conflicts of interest; disclose potential conflicts to Manager and HR within 5 business days of awareness.
5. Do not accept gifts above INR 2,000 value from vendors or customers without written CFO/Compliance approval.
6. Use company systems and AI tools only in accordance with POL-SEC and POL-AI policies.
7. Report suspected misconduct in good faith through Manager, HR, Compliance, or the designated reporting channel.

## 3. Consequences
Violations may result in coaching, written warning, suspension, or termination under POL-HR-005, and where applicable referral to external authorities.
