## 1. Purpose
Establish clear authority levels for expenditure approval, invoice processing, and budget variances to ensure fiscal control and audit readiness.

## 2. Approval Thresholds

| Amount (USD) | Required Approver | Secondary | Notes |
|--------------|-------------------|-----------|-------|
| ≤ 2,500 | Department Manager | — | Auto-route if under budget |
| 2,501 – 15,000 | Department Director | Finance Analyst | |
| 15,001 – 50,000 | VP / Business Unit Head | Controller | |
| 50,001 – 150,000 | CFO | CEO (notify) | Board report quarterly |
| > 150,000 | CEO + CFO | Board Finance Committee | Formal resolution |

## 3. Invoice Processing Rules
- All invoices must reference a valid Purchase Order (PO) for amounts ≥ $5,000.
- Duplicate invoice numbers for the same vendor are prohibited.
- Invoices older than 90 days require special justification.
- Any variance > 5% between PO and invoice requires re-approval at original threshold.

## 4. Budget Variance Authority
- Department heads may reallocate within their cost centers up to 8% of annual budget without escalation.
- Variances > 8% or any capital expenditure variance require Controller review.
- Year-end forecast variances > 10% escalate to CFO.

## 5. Segregation of Duties
- Requestor cannot approve their own expenditure.
- AP processor cannot be the final approver for the same transaction.
- System access to create vendors is restricted to Finance Operations with dual control.

## 6. Exceptions
Emergency purchases (safety, production stoppage) may be ratified post-facto within 5 business days under the Emergency Purchase Protocol (FIN-POL-EMG-002).
