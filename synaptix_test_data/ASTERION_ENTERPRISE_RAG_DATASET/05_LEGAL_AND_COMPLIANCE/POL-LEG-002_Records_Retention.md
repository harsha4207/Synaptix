# Records Retention and Confidentiality

**Document ID:** POL-LEG-002  
**Version:** 1.5 | **Status:** Approved | **Effective:** 2025-01-01 | **Owner:** Chief Compliance Officer | **Org:** ORG-ASTERION-001

## 1. Purpose
Define minimum retention periods and confidentiality handling for major record categories.

## 2. Retention Categories (Synthetic Internal Schedule)
| Category | Retention | Classification Default | Owner |
|----------|-----------|------------------------|-------|
| Employee personnel file | Employment + 7 years | Confidential | HR |
| Financial transactions & ledgers | 8 years | Confidential | Finance |
| Contracts (executed) | Term + 7 years | Confidential | Legal |
| Security incident records | 5 years | Restricted | CISO |
| Policy versions | Permanent (archive) | Internal | Compliance |
| Support tickets | 3 years | Internal | Customer Support |

Deletion or anonymization after retention requires documented approval of the data owner and Compliance.

## 3. Confidentiality Agreements
Employees acknowledge confidentiality obligations at joining (SOP-HR-002). Customer and vendor NDAs are managed by Legal.
