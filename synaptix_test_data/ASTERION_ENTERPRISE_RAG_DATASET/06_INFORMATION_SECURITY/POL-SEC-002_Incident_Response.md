# Security Incident Response Policy

**Document ID:** POL-SEC-002  
**Version:** 3.0 | **Status:** Approved | **Effective:** 2025-01-01 | **Owner:** CISO | **Org:** ORG-ASTERION-001

## 1. Purpose
Define detection, reporting, containment, investigation, and closure of security incidents.

## 2. Incident Severity
| Severity | Description | Initial Response SLA | Notify |
|----------|-------------|----------------------|--------|
| Critical | Active breach, ransomware, large-scale data exposure | 15 minutes | CISO, CIO, CEO, Legal |
| High | Confirmed unauthorized access, targeted malware | 1 hour | CISO, CIO |
| Medium | Suspicious activity, failed intrusion attempts at scale | 4 hours | Security Manager |
| Low | Policy violation without data loss, isolated phishing click | 1 business day | Security team |

## 3. Reporting
All employees must report suspected incidents to security@example.com or the designated portal within 1 hour of discovery. Do not attempt personal investigation of malware.
