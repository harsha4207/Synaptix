# Business Process Policy
**LumenForge Products Inc.**  
**Document ID:** BUS-POL-001  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Effective:** 2025-01-01

## 1. Order-to-Cash
- All orders require valid customer segment and region coding.
- Discounts > 15% require Sales Director approval.
- Returns must be logged within 30 days of delivery; Return_Flag set in transaction system.

## 2. Inventory Management
- Reorder points reviewed quarterly.
- SKUs with Days_of_Supply > 45 days trigger Overstock review.
- Below-reorder items escalate to Procurement within 2 business days.

## 3. Supply Chain Incident Handling
- Any delivery delay > 5 days is logged as an incident.
- Root cause and resolution date mandatory before closure.
- Recurring carrier or region issues escalate to quarterly business review.

## 4. Data Integrity for Analytics
All transactional and inventory data in this workspace is synthetic and maintained for SYNAPTIX COMMAND prototype testing. Real commercial data must not be mixed into these files.
