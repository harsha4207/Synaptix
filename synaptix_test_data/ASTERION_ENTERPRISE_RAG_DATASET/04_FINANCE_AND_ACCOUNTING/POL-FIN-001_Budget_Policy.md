# Budget Planning and Control Policy

**Document ID:** POL-FIN-001  
**Version:** 3.0 | **Status:** Approved | **Effective:** 2025-04-01 | **Review:** 2026-03-31  
**Owner:** CFO | **Classification:** Internal | **Org:** ORG-ASTERION-001

## 1. Purpose
Define the annual budgeting cycle, ownership, revision rules, and variance monitoring.

## 2. Budget Cycle
| Milestone | Target Date (each FY) | Owner |
|-----------|----------------------|-------|
| Budget guidelines issued | 15 December | CFO |
| Department submissions due | 31 January | Department Heads |
| ELT review | February | ELT |
| Board approval (synthetic) | March | CEO / Board |
| Final budgets loaded | 25 March | Finance |
| FY start | 1 April | — |

## 3. Ownership
Each Department Head owns their department budget. Finance provides templates, consolidation, and challenge.

## 4. Revisions
Mid-year revisions require CFO approval if the net change exceeds 5% of the department budget. Revisions are versioned and effective from the first day of the following month.

## 5. Variance Reporting
- Monthly variance pack by Finance Manager to Department Heads (by 10th business day).
- Material variances (>8% or > INR 200,000) require written explanation and action plan within 5 business days.
