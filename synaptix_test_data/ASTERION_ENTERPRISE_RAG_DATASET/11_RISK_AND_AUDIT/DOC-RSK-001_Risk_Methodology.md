## 1. Scoring
- **Likelihood:** 1 (Rare) – 5 (Almost Certain)
- **Impact:** 1 (Insignificant) – 5 (Severe)
- **Inherent Risk Score:** Likelihood × Impact (range 1–25)
- **Severity Bands:** 1–4 Low; 5–9 Medium; 10–16 High; 17–25 Critical

## 2. Residual Risk
After documenting existing controls, residual likelihood and impact are assessed; residual score = residual likelihood × residual impact.

## 3. Escalation
- Critical residual risks: escalate to ELT within 5 business days.
- High residual risks: mitigation plan with owner and target date required within 15 business days.
