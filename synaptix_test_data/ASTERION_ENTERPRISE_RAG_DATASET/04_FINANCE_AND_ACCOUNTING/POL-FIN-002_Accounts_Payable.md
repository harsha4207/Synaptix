# Accounts Payable and Invoice Processing Policy

**Document ID:** POL-FIN-002  
**Version:** 2.5 | **Status:** Approved | **Effective:** 2025-01-01 | **Review:** 2026-12-31  
**Owner:** CFO | **Classification:** Internal | **Org:** ORG-ASTERION-001

## 1. Purpose
Ensure timely, accurate, and controlled payment of vendor obligations.

## 2. Invoice Requirements
Every invoice must contain: vendor legal name, vendor ID (from master), invoice number, date, PO number (if ≥ INR 5,000), description, amount, tax, bank details matching vendor master.

## 3. Three-Way Match
For PO-based invoices ≥ INR 25,000: system match of PO, goods/service receipt, and invoice. Exceptions require Finance Manager approval.

## 4. Payment Terms
Standard: Net 30 from invoice receipt date unless contract specifies otherwise. Early-payment discounts may be taken if beneficial.

## 5. Duplicate Detection
Finance system flags exact invoice number + vendor ID duplicates. Near-duplicates (±2% amount, same vendor, within 14 days) require manual review before payment.
