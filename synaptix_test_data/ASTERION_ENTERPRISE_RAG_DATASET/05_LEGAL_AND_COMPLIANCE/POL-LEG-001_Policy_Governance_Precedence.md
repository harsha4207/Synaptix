## 1. Purpose
Define how policies are created, approved, versioned, superseded, and how conflicts between documents are resolved.

## 2. Policy Lifecycle
1. Draft created by owning department.
2. Review by Legal/Compliance for conflict and clarity.
3. Approval by designated Approver Role.
4. Publication with Effective Date; prior version marked Superseded with Effective-To date.
5. Periodic review by Review Date.

## 3. Precedence Hierarchy (Internal Documents)
When two internal documents conflict:
1. **Currently effective Approved policy** of the higher authority (CEO/Board > C-level policy > Department policy).
2. More specific policy controls over general policy for the same subject.
3. Later Effective Date controls over earlier if both Approved and overlapping.
4. SOP cannot override Policy; SOP implements Policy.
5. Draft, Retired, or Archived documents have **no operational force**.

Unresolved conflicts after applying the hierarchy must be escalated to the Policy Owner of the higher document and, if needed, to the Chief Compliance Officer within 5 business days. Do **not** silently pick one interpretation.

## 4. External Law vs Internal Policy
Genuine legal or regulatory obligations (where verified) take precedence over internal policy. Internal policy may be stricter than law but cannot authorize non-compliance with applicable law. This dataset contains **synthetic internal controls** and does not invent statutory text. For real-world legal questions, qualified legal counsel is required.

## 5. Authority Levels for RAG
| Authority Level | Meaning |
|-----------------|---------|
| Authoritative | Currently Approved policy or SOP with Effective Date covering the query date |
| Historical | Superseded version valid for a past date range |
| Informational | Guides, examples, FAQs — not binding rules |
| Draft | Not approved — must not be treated as current rule |
