# Procurement Policy

**Document ID:** POL-PRC-001  
**Version:** 3.0 | **Status:** Approved | **Effective:** 2025-04-01 | **Owner:** Head of Procurement | **Org:** ORG-ASTERION-001

## 1. Purpose
Ensure purchases are necessary, competitively sourced where required, properly approved, and recorded against the correct cost center and vendor.

## 2. Vendor Onboarding
New vendors require: legal name verification, tax identifiers, bank details, conflict-of-interest declaration, and (for spend > INR 500,000 annualized) basic due-diligence checklist completed by Procurement.

## 3. Purchase Requisition to PO
1. Requester creates requisition with cost center, justification, and estimated amount.
2. Approval per POL-FIN-003 PO thresholds.
3. Procurement creates PO only after approval and vendor master validation.
4. Goods/services receipt recorded before invoice payment (three-way match rules in POL-FIN-002).
