# Employee Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Document ID:** POL-HR-003  
**Version:** 4.0 | **Status:** Approved | **Effective:** 2025-04-01 | **Review:** 2026-03-31  
**Owner:** CHRO (policy) / CFO (financial limits) | **Classification:** Internal | **Org:** ORG-ASTERION-001

## 1. Purpose
Define when employees may travel, what costs are reimbursable, documentation requirements, and approval thresholds. Financial approval limits are authoritative in POL-FIN-003; this policy defines eligibility and process.

## 2. Pre-Travel Approval
| Trip Type | Approver | Advance Booking Rule |
|-----------|----------|----------------------|
| Domestic, < INR 25,000 estimated | Direct Manager | Prefer 7+ days advance |
| Domestic, ≥ INR 25,000 | Department Head | 10+ days advance preferred |
| International | Department Head + CFO (or delegate) | Written business case required |

## 3. Reimbursable Categories and Limits (INR)
| Category | Limit / Rule | Receipt Required |
|----------|--------------|------------------|
| Airfare (domestic) | Economy; lowest logical fare | Yes |
| Hotel (metro) | Max INR 6,000 / night | Yes |
| Hotel (non-metro) | Max INR 4,000 / night | Yes |
| Local conveyance | Actuals; prefer company-approved vendors | Yes if > INR 500 |
| Meals (per day, own) | INR 1,200 | Yes if > INR 500 total |
| Client entertainment | Pre-approved; max INR 3,000 / person | Yes + attendee list |
| Internet / incidentals | Actuals up to INR 500 / day | Yes |

Limits are per person per night/day. Higher limits require pre-approval by Finance Manager or above and must be recorded on the travel request.

## 4. Submission and Payment
- Claims must be submitted within **15 calendar days** of return or expense date.
- Required: original/ digital receipts, approved travel request ID, cost center.
- Payment SLA: 10 business days after complete submission and approval.
- Approval of the claim amount follows POL-FIN-003 thresholds (Manager / Director / CFO bands).

## 5. Non-Reimbursable
Personal side-trips, mini-bar, spa, traffic fines, premium cabin upgrades without prior written approval, expenses lacking receipts above INR 500.
