# SOP — Expense Claim Processing

**Document ID:** SOP-FIN-001  
**Version:** 1.5 | **Status:** Approved | **Effective:** 2025-04-01  
**Owner:** Finance Manager | **Related Policy:** POL-FIN-003, POL-HR-003 | **Org:** ORG-ASTERION-001

## 1. Purpose
Steps from claim submission to payment or rejection.

## 2. Ordered Steps
1. Employee submits claim in expense system with receipts, cost center, and travel request ID if applicable.
2. System validates mandatory fields and flags amounts above Manager self-approval band.
3. Claim routes per POL-FIN-003 thresholds.
4. Approver reviews (business need, limits, receipts) and approves/rejects within 5 business days.
5. On approval, AP schedules payment per payment run calendar (typically weekly).
6. Payment confirmation posted; employee notified.

## 3. Completion Criteria
Status = Paid or Rejected; GL posting complete for paid claims.

## 4. SLA
Approval: 5 business days. Payment after approval: within 10 business days.
